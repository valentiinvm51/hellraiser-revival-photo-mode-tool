## 🎮 Photo Tips for Hellraiser: Revival

The game rewards patience. These habits help you get better results with any capture setup.

- **Use the light, don't fight it.** Hellraiser: Revival leans on strong directional light and deep shadow. Position your camera so the light source grazes your subject rather than sitting behind it.
- **Frame with architecture.** Doorways, archways, and stone corridors make natural frames that pull the eye toward the center of the image.
- **Get low.** A camera placed slightly below eye level makes environments feel larger and more imposing.
- **Wait for motion to settle.** Fog, dust, and particles look best in a still frame when they have had a moment to spread out.
- **Shoot the details.** Chains, carved surfaces, and ornate props are where the art direction shines. Close-up profiles are worth keeping for this reason alone.
- **Take more shots than you need.** Storage is cheap and the perfect frame is often the tenth attempt, not the first.
